Owner: Why did those teeth go bad?
Dr. Kim: Daisy has moderate to advanced gum disease. Plaque and tartar built up under the gums, and bacteria got into the roots of those teeth. It's very common at her age and it's why her breath was so bad. The anesthesia went smoothly and there were no complications.
Owner: What does she need to take?
Dr. Kim: Two medicines. First, carprofen for pain and swelling. It's a 25 milligram tablet, one tablet twice a day with food for five days. Second, gabapentin for comfort. It's a 100 milligram capsule, one capsule every twelve hours for three days. Please don't give any other pain medicine along with the carprofen.
Owner: And what about food?
Dr. Kim: Tonight offer a small meal, about a quarter of her normal amount, and some water. For the next ten days feed only soft food, canned food or kibble soaked in warm water. No bones, rawhide or hard chews for two weeks. Keep her calm and quiet tonight, because she may be wobbly from the sedation. Don't brush her teeth for two weeks, then we'll start a gentle brushing routine.
Owner: What should I watch out for?
Dr. Kim: A few drops of pink saliva are normal for a day or two. Call us if you see bleeding that doesn't stop, swelling of her face, if she won't eat for more than a day, if she vomits more than once, or if she seems very tired or paws at her face. If it's after hours, call the emergency clinic number on the sheet.
